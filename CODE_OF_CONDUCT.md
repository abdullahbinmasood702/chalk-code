# Code of Conduct

ChalkCode is a project built for students, by students. Everyone taking part — filing issues, opening pull requests, joining discussions — is expected to help keep it a welcoming space.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to coding or open source.
- Give constructive feedback; focus on the work, not the person.
- Accept that people contribute in their free time and may not respond immediately.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior in this project, open an issue, or contact the maintainer directly through the contact details on the [Code with ABM](https://codewithabm.vercel.app/) website.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, or pull requests that don't align with this Code of Conduct, and may block contributors for repeated or serious violations.
